## In one line

A cozy incremental game about the collectible caps of the '90s: slam your stack, flip caps to earn coins, grow the pile to 108, open packs and collect 216 grotesque caps across eight series. Beat the champion of every district and become King of the Yard.

## About the game

**Slam. Flip. Collect.**

Mortal Caps is a cozy incremental game about the collectible caps of the '90s. Every run is a few seconds of pure satisfaction: drop your stack on the table, hit it with your slammer and watch the caps flip. Every flipped cap pays out, and every coin goes into making the next slam even bigger.

## Key features

- **A stack that grows.** Start with a handful of caps and build up to 108: a thicker stack, wider slams, a heavier hand, crits and long combos.
- **A big upgrade tree.** Dozens of upgrades across eight districts: golden caps, a helper friend, aftershocks, poppers and more.
- **216 caps to collect.** Eight series — Robots, Mutants, Racers, Sea Monsters, Space Pirates, Dinosaurs, Bugs and Kaiju — in four rarities with holo, prism and legendary finishes.
- **Packs and a collector's magazine.** Tear open foil packs, hunt for legendaries and slammers, and fill every issue of the magazine.
- **13 slammers with powers.** Chain lightning, cannon volleys, a T-rex roar and more.
- **8 champions.** Beat the best player in every district and become King of the Yard.
- **'90s grotesque art.** Monsters in the spirit of the caps from the schoolyard.
